# Cloud Service Providers

FedRAMP is a security framework for businesses to set security goals for themselves, continuously validate the effectiveness of the capabilities used to meet those goals, measure their performance against those goals, and ensure security and engineering teams have the resources necessary to meet those goals. It should not be treated like a traditional compliance framework.

## Eligibility for FedRAMP Certification

To be listed in the FedRAMP Marketplace and qualify for FedRAMP Certification, cloud services must have one of the following government-wide use cases:

1. **Direct Government-Wide Use:** The service will be used directly by multiple federal agency customers for integration into federal information systems that fall within the scope of 44 USC § 3506.

2. **Indirect Government-Wide Use:** The service will be used as a third-party information resource in other cloud services that have direct government-wide use.

!!! danger "FedRAMP does not support or provide 'equivalency.'"

    The Department of War established the [Cybersecurity Maturity Model Certification](https://dodcio.defense.gov/CMMC/) (CMMC) to enhance cybersecurity protections for sensitive unclassified information within the Defense Industrial Base (DIB).
    CMMC requirements apply to private companies that do business with the Department of War and establishes requirements that are only relevant to the Department of War.

    All questions about "FedRAMP Equivalency" or the application of FedRAMP Certification requirements for CMMC should be directed to the Department of War. FedRAMP is only able to support Certification for cloud services with direct or indirect government-wide use.

## Limited Program Certification for Rev5

FedRAMP is making Program Certification available for Rev5 in extremely limited circumstances in the FedRAMP 20x Phase 4 Rev5 Program Closeout initiative. This initiative is intended to provide a Rev5 option for a very limited subset of cloud services who have heavily invested into FedRAMP Rev5 but were unable to complete their Agency Certification due to circumstances outside their control.

This initiative includes strict eligibility requirements, mandatory full CR26 adoption, and a case-by-case application and review. Cloud services must be able to demonstrate that their service is intended to be used by federal agencies directly or inside cloud services used by federal agencies directly.

More information about the Rev5 Program Closeout initiative, including deadlines and eligibility criteria, are available on the [FedRAMP 20x Phase 4 micro-site](https://www.fedramp.gov/20x/phase4).
